This project was originally made to help organize due dates and improve time management.
With many note-taking apps, I always felt like I was not able to organize both due dates and daily goals into one calendar, especially since I sometimes don't have a dedicated time for things, just a goal to complete them.
The goal of this app is to give a place where you can write down general due dates and mark other important dates, then add more specific notes for each day without the restrictions of a normal digital calendar.
